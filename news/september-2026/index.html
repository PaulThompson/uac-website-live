UAC team, Perisher Cup – championed by Ned Dale and Talia Davis.

Stay up to date with the latest news from the University Alpine Club, including lodge operations, upcoming member events, governance initiatives, and regional snow industry updates.

Key updates

Board & strategic initiatives

  • The Board continues to focus on lease clarification, constitution and governance reviews, strategic planning, lodge operations, and preparations for the club’s 75th anniversary celebrations.
  • Several member-led subcommittees have been established to drive these initiatives forward.

Lodge operations & lease

  • Discussions with NSW National Parks regarding future lease arrangements have commenced.
  • Current lease terms remain unchanged while broader reviews of valley lodges are completed.
  • Winter bookings remained strong despite challenging snow conditions, and summer bookings are now open through February 2027.

75th anniversary celebration

  • Save the date: Saturday, 15 May 2027.
  • The Club’s 75th Anniversary Dinner will be held at Park Royal Darling Harbour, Sydney.
  • Members interested in helping organise the event are encouraged to get involved.

Upcoming work parties

  • The first building and maintenance work party is scheduled for 31 October to 1 November.
  • Additional building and house preparation weekends are planned for early 2027.

New UAC logo consultation

  • Members are invited to provide feedback and submit logo concepts as part of the Club’s 75th anniversary branding initiative.
  • Design submissions close 10 October.

Perisher Cup success

  • UAC competed in the annual Perisher Cup on 12 September, finishing an impressive 5th out of 13 teams.
  • The event continues a long tradition of friendly competition between Perisher Valley lodges.

Regional snow industry news

  • Updates include snowmaking developments, resort infrastructure investments, lease renewal discussions through SLOPES, and changes across the broader Snowy Mountains region.

Member contributions wanted

  • The Club is seeking newsletter contributors and members interested in helping develop UAC’s social media presence.
