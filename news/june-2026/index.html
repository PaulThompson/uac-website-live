June 2026

2 June 2026

Spring has given way to the crisp promise of winter. As the mountains ready themselves, so too does your new Board — energised, grateful for your confidence, and focused on the season and year ahead. We have much to share.

The lodge in 1953…

…and in July 2025.

New board elected at the April EGM

At the Annual General Meeting held in April 2026 the Chairman was elected unopposed however, there were 13 other members registered for a director’s position. The voting process which was used resulted in voting by 118 members of the 155 eligible to vote for the Board of six Directors. The extent of the voting member participation was unprecedented. The due diligence & transparency shown during the election process shows the depth of professionalism, dedication & stewardship within the outgoing/interim board – our thanks go to all those involved.

Your new Board comprises:

  • Chairperson – David Stuart
  • Secretary – Bill Russell
  • Treasurer – Stewart Smith
  • Ordinary Directors – Lesley Neilson (also Membership Registrar), Talia Davis, Ned Dale & John Lembke

From your Board

With appointment on the 24th April, the new board has identified a revised newsletter with bi-monthly distribution and regular distribution of information & events on a revised and updated web site.

Initial priorities include:

  • Automated booking system implementation. Ultimately this system will encompass membership management and administrative systems.
  • Lease renewal preparations to ensure the club’s long-term security.
  • 75th Anniversary celebrations in April 2027.
  • Online presence and communications for members with improved connection year-round, typically the newsletter & website.
  • Sub-committees to be identified to support & enhance the timely implementation of Board & Club matters.

Membership

Life membership award

In recognition of their exceptional and sustained service to the club as members of the Board, David Smith and John Russell Jnr have been awarded Life Membership — the highest honour the club can bestow. We are deeply grateful for everything they have given to UAC and to the community it fosters.

Recent passing of two of our club’s well-known identities

Anthony “Peter” Melhuish: 1931–2026, a life member who actively participated in the club for 70 years. Fostered scout involvement at UAC for many years, avid cross country skier and was our booking officer for over 30 years diligently organising members’ accommodation needs professionally and diplomatically.

Harley James Lytton Wright: 1944–2026, a member’s spouse who dedicated so much of his life’s enthusiasm to UAC. A very proficient back country skier he was always open to discussion about his love of nature and the environment. Harley was always prepared to “roll up his sleeve” and be involved with work at the lodge.

Both funerals were well represented by club members with UAC’s heartfelt condolences passed onto each family.

Lodge operations

Lease update

Information provided by SLOPES (Ski Lodges Organisation of Perisher, Smiggins & Guthega) advises that most of the valley lodges’ leases expire on 30 June 2028 (Source SLOPES 10/4/26). Key dates:

  • July 2026 – NPWS appoints their valuer to determine lodge base annual rent to the NPWS.
  • 1 February 2027 – NPWS notifies Club lessees of its assessment of MRP, BRP and TRP.
  • 1 April 2027 – Lessees to lodge a dispute notice no later than this date (if they wish).
  • 1 July 2027 to 31 December 2027 – lease option exercise period.

UAC lease expires 31 December 2029. Ongoing discussions with SLOPES will determine the time frame applicable to our lodge. Further information will be disseminated to members as this comes to hand.

Work party: building & house – 2026

Over the past few months there has been a couple of work parties to ensure our lodge is fully functional for the next twelve months, in particular the winter ski season.

Due to the AGM elections, those in January and April were convened at short notice to store wood, undertake external waterproofing, stone pointing & pipe insulating.

The early May weekend combined building and house work party was a full complement of members with all the scheduled cleaning, food stock and maintenance work completed. An audit of the building was undertaken to identify work for next summer along with regular maintenance items.

All future work party dates will be advertised well in advance to ensure members can diarise the dates.

Celebration party with food and drinks after a long work day!

Club procedures updated

Over the summer the hut opening/closing and general procedures have been updated to better reflect changes, typically rubbish collection/recycling, timer operation, contact details for emergency etc. A hard copy is now located on the notice board and a digital version will soon be uploaded to the web site for easy reference.

Census night – 11 August 2026

The Australian Bureau of Statistics Census counts every person in Australia on Census night, wherever they are. If you are staying at the lodge on Tuesday 11 August 2026, please ensure you have a device available — a smartphone, tablet or laptop — so you can complete your Census forms online. Forms can be completed at abs.gov.au/census.

Valley and area news

Perisher’s Sundeck sells

With winter approaching the Perisher Sundeck Hotel has been sold. Built in 1959 and after almost 30 years owned by Cliff Wallis the business has been purchased by Peter Dean.

Sotero Medical Centre – Perisher

Operators for this year have been announced. Opening hours are Mon–Sun 8.30am till 6pm, and night skiing Tue/Sat 5pm till 9pm.

New Jindabyne Adventure Park

As part of the Jindabyne NSW Government $196m investment, a “Jindabyne Mountain Bike & Adventure Park” is to commence stage one within the next two years.

Perisher trail map for the new “Epic Perisher Beginner Bundle”

Following on from the success of the Epic Australia 4-Day Pass in 2025, we are very excited to launch a new Epic Australia Pass product this season – the Epic Beginner Bundle!

This is an exciting opportunity that makes skiing and boarding more accessible for those new to the snow. The all-in-one Epic Beginner Bundle is designed for adult beginner skiers and snowboarders (18+).

This product will include:

  • Three flexible days of beginner lift access, three group lessons, and three days of rentals at Perisher, Falls Creek and Hotham for adults (18+) across both skiing and snowboarding.
  • There are no blackout dates with the Epic Beginner Bundle, and the three days do not need to be used consecutively or at the same mountain.
  • The Epic Beginner Bundle costs just $499 (that’s $167 per day).
  • 2026 Epic Beginner Bundle purchasers will receive 20% off the following season’s Adult Epic Australia Pass or Adult Epic Australia 4-Day Pass for the 2027/2028 season.
