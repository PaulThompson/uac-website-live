A proposed new look for the UAC website

22 October 2026

A new version of the club website is being trialled. You can try it now at paulthompson.github.io/uac-website-live. It could replace the current site at uac.net.au.

What’s in the trial

All the content from the current site has been carried across and tidied up:

  • About the lodge – lodge info and a photo gallery (tap a photo to view it full size)
  • Accommodation rates – this year’s rates and season dates, with a phone-friendly card for each membership category
  • Book a stay – straight links to Clubman for bookings and availability, the Clubman FAQs, and the booking form for those still using the old method
  • Membership – membership types and moving from Student to Full membership
  • News – every newsletter in one place, readable on the web with photos, plus a PDF where there is one

The trial site works properly on phones and tablets, loads quickly even on a patchy mountain connection, and supports dark mode. Existing links and bookmarks would keep working after a switch.

Less spam

Club email addresses still appear and work as normal, but they’re hidden from the bots that collect addresses for spam.

Behind the scenes

The current site runs on WordPress, which needs regular updates, plugins and logins to keep it secure. The proposed site would be much simpler:

  • Clubman would continue to handle bookings, just as it does now.
  • GitHub Pages could host the website and its photos for free.
  • GitHub keeps a copy of every change ever made, so nothing gets lost and any mistake can be undone.
  • Markdown is a simple way of writing text. Each page and newsletter is just a short text document.
  • Astro turns those documents into the website. There’s no database or server behind it, so there’s very little to look after and very little that could be hacked.

Updating the rates each year could be as simple as typing the new numbers into a form.

How would editing work?

Committee members could update the site from any web browser, with no technical knowledge needed and no special account.

  1. Get an invite. Paul will send you an invitation by email.
  2. Open the editor. Go to

app.pagescms.org/paulthompson/uac-website/main/collection/news to see the newsletters, and click Add an entry to write a new one. The menu on the left also lists the other pages, the rates and the photo gallery. 3. Fill in the form. There are simple text boxes for the title, date and summary, and a writing area for the main content. You can upload photos and PDFs straight from your computer or phone. 4. Save. Your changes would appear on the website about a minute later. If a newsletter isn’t ready yet, tick Draft and it stays hidden until you untick it.

Every change is recorded with the name of the person who made it, and any mistake can be undone.

Have your say

Please take a look and send any feedback to Paul.

Most of the gallery photos date from 2013–14. If you have recent photos of the lodge, the view or members enjoying their stay, please send them in too, and let us know if they include people who’d rather not appear online.
