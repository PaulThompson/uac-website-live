Perisher Valley · Kosciuszko National Park

Ski and relax at Perisher

The UAC lodge is located just a short ski or walk from the Front Valley lifts and the Perisher Centre. It’s a friendly, member-run lodge with a large common room and kitchen looking out over the mountains – the perfect place to relax after a good day on the snow.

  • 16beds in 8 twin rooms
  • 1952member-owned since
  • Short walkto Front Valley lifts
  • 150+members
