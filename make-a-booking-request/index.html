Clubman FAQs

I didn’t get an activation/invite email – how do I log in?

Check your junk/spam folders for the email – there will also be an announcement of Clubman’s go-live that came from the same email address. If you can’t locate this email, please contact email address hidden (enable JavaScript) to make sure we have the correct email address for you when we set up your Clubman login.

My spouse/partner did not receive an invite – do they get an account?

Yes, Spouse Members also get a Clubman account and have the same booking rights as members. We have uploaded all members, and all Spouse Members who had a booking this year – if you do not have a booking for this year this may be why you have not received your invite. It could also be that the email address for your spouse was incorrect or missing in our records. In both cases, please email email address hidden (enable JavaScript) to get this resolved.

Can I still pay by bank transfer?

Once we are solely operating bookings through Clubman, we will only be offering direct debit and credit card payments. For now, while we are all still getting the hang of the system, the old booking method is still available – see below.

Old booking method

While we get used to Clubman, the old booking method of form and bank transfer remains available.

  1. Download and complete the UAC Booking Form.
  2. By making a booking application you are agreeing to the terms and conditions in the Detailed Booking Information. This document is due to be updated shortly to align it with Clubman.
  3. The UAC banking details can be found in the booking form or in the Detailed Booking Information.
  4. See the accommodation rates for pricing.
  5. Your booking is confirmed once the Booking Manager has contacted you and payment has been received.
