About the lodge

UAC is a friendly 16 bed lodge, for members, located a short ski/walk to Perisher Centre, the Front Valley lifts and the Mt Perisher chairlifts.

The ski lodge

The lodge has 8 rooms, all with twin beds. Blankets and pillows are provided, however each visitor is required to bring their own sheets and pillow cases for the duration of their stay. There is a small laundry in the basement should this be required.

All other facilities are shared. The large common room and kitchen have magnificent views over the mountains and are a great place to enjoy a quiet afternoon reading, a glass of wine or a quiet ale at the end of a good day’s skiing. A selection of games and books are provided and available to be shared during your stay.
